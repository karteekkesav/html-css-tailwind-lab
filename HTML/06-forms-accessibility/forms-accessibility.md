# Forms Accessibility

## What It Means

Accessible forms are easy to understand, navigate, complete, and correct using keyboards and assistive technologies.

## Interview Answer

I make forms accessible by using visible labels, connecting labels with inputs, choosing the right input types, grouping related fields with `fieldset` and `legend`, adding clear validation messages, and ensuring the form works with keyboard navigation.

## Common Mistakes

- Placeholder-only labels.
- Error messages that are only color-based.
- Missing `fieldset` and `legend` for grouped options.
- Custom controls without keyboard support.

## Quick Revision

Every form control needs a name, purpose, state, and clear feedback.
